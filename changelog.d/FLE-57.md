### Added

- **FLE-57: instances and jobs, operator side** (milestone M0.04, wave 5). `fleet instance:*` and `fleet job:*` commands (deploy, redeploy, start, stop, destroy, list, show, log, retry), the Instances list with a deploy form, the instance detail page with its actions, and the Jobs pages with the live log (Mercure, per-job topic; the masked log text is fetched from `/jobs/{id}/log`), retry and history. Destroy is administrators only; a member sees only their projects' instances and jobs. Installer and units: one job worker `fleet-worker@1` (the SQLite queue takes a single consumer), the status watcher `fleet-status.service` (keeps instance states true to `ddev list` and `docker stats`) and `fleet-boot.service` (starts the instances after a reboot), plus a job retention setting. Dev and test only: a fake job for the browser specs. Playwright: instance and job specs, live log, phone width and member access.
